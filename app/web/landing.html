GPS leaves the photo

Coordinates are read once and kept in an access-limited table keyed by pole code. Every stored copy is re-encoded without them, as RA 10173 requires.

Limits that follow the weather

Give span, temperature and wind, and the allowable sag follows the change-of-state equation. Heat lengthens the wire; wind adds load (Eq. 9).
